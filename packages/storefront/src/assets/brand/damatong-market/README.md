# Damatong Market brand assets

These SVG files are the source-of-truth identity for the separately managed Damatong sales Channel.
They must not replace the MOYAO AI default-site assets.

- `app-icon.svg`: compact mark for app-icon and square-logo positions.
- `logo-on-light.svg`: wordmark for light storefront surfaces.
- `logo-on-dark.svg`: wordmark for dark campaign surfaces.

The mark combines a shop entrance with a connected route. The restrained marketplace palette uses
brand navy `#102d4a`, action blue `#2f6feb`, promotional orange `#f28c28`, cool off-white
`#f5f7fb`, and white. Green and muted violet remain semantic accents inside the storefront only.
